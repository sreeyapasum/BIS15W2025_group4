🍸 Alcohol Effects Analysis 🥂

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the effects of alcohol on the human body using statistical methods and data analysis techniques. Our team explored various physiological and behavioral impacts of alcohol consumption through research, data collection, and analysis.

📊 Dataset

We utilized data from the CDC Alcohol-Related Disease Impact (ARDI) tool (link to dataset), which includes data on alcohol-attributable deaths and years of potential life lost due to excessive alcohol use as well as alcohol being a major contributing factor for other diseases.

🎯 Project Goals

🔍 Investigate the correlation between alcohol intake and physiological effects.

🧹 Clean, process, and visualize the dataset.

📈 Apply statistical methods to analyze trends and patterns.

🏥 Interpret findings and discuss implications for public health.
